This path remains clear of the separate modeled helper and open-DW/loader envelopes.

The east side is not promoted as another full entrance: top-to-cleanup is 25 inches and the occupied east stool is 28 inches from cleanup. Its relationship to the family-room/M5 approach remains a limitation to check; M5 is a point/direction reference, not a complete measured opening envelope. The west path is the primary kitchen approach, not a substituted hall route.

Behind guests, the nearest assumed table-chair gap is 18.4 inches (44.4 from guest counter edge to that chair). The primary route avoids that gap, but movement between guest seats and table chairs remains constrained. Both chair sets pulled simultaneously overlap. Four occasional places therefore still involve coordinated chair movement.

## P2 — stair-side working elbow

Retain the upper 36 × 24 mise landing, extend a 30-inch-deep run another 30.5 inches beside the enclosed stair side, and join directly into a 90 × 42 social/helper return at X6–96, Y166–208. This is a full-width working elbow, without the prior shallow diagonal connector.

The extension adds 915 square inches, about **6.4 sq ft**, and takes range-left working-edge frontage from 24 to 54.5 inches. The 30-inch depth refers to the new segment; the retained upper segment is 36 inches deep. Its left edge sits just outside the captured bottom-tread right-edge line. The higher stair, enclosure thickness, vertical clearances and countertop support remain unmeasured; this is proposed usable surface, not demonstrated reclaimable construction space.

Four places use **three along the far edge plus one at the free end**, not two occupied ends. The middle and right far-side places are everyday seats; the left far-side stool and end stool are brought out for hosting. All three far-side frontages are 30 inches. Reserved knee pockets avoid the central 42 × 18 helper zone, with a 24 × 18 board. This arrangement keeps the attached end structurally useful and avoids pretending it is a free seating end.

The occupied end seat is about 45.5 inches from cleanup, replacing the earlier 17-inch counter-tip constraint with a materially better free-end approach. Island to the standing helper proxy is about 42.1 inches. Separate helper work remains out of the principal chef-to-cooktop band, though actual movements around the enlarged landing need mock-up verification.

There are two essential connection assumptions:

1. A 36-inch clear stair-base door/opening test, at the near wall of the old porch. Actual jambs and any required widening are unverified.
2. Approximately 48 inches of clear opening at the stair-side end of the table-facing porch partition, from conditional Y228.9 to Y277. Retained piers, structural feasibility and floor levels are unverified.

The serving path deliberately goes through this newly opened connection. The old porch remains a breakfast/beverage destination; no guest seats are moved into it. The new giant screened porch at M5 remains distinct and unchanged.

With the table chair in its normal kitchen-side position, the nearest gap behind seated guests is only 17.4 inches. **P2's tested 36-inch occupied route requires parking that one chair for hosting.** With it parked, the gap to the fixed tabletop is 36.4 inches and a specific 36-inch swept path can be drawn through both proposed openings, behind the guests and around the free end. ## Recommendation

Advance F2 first if straightforward daily movement is the priority. Keep P2 as a serious alternative if the additional mise is worth making the old-porch connection integral to circulation and parking one table chair during roughly 20 hosting occasions a year. The next decision is about those explicit dependencies, not another set of angled surfaces.

Before finer cabinetry, a tape mock-up should check F2's west end stool and family-room-side stool in their actual occupied positions. For P2, the consequential checks are the stair-side section and clear, level passage through the two proposed porch openings. No repeat room survey or workflow interview is needed.

## Verification and reproduction

The model records all polygons and exact tested centerlines. `geometry.py` computes polygon/segment distances; `build.py` generates the two cases; `routes.py` checks the entire line segments against modeled obstacles, not only waypoint distances. Minima are stored in `explorations-model.json`. These calculations expose model constraints, not building-code or accessibility certification.

Run `python3 build.py`, then `python3 routes.py`. `baseline.json` is a preserved snapshot of the corrected table/core from the preceding study. Replace the template's `__MODEL__` token with the generated JSON to rebuild the fragment. The standalone HTML is a portable rendered copy. Browser checks exercised every operating state, chair toggle and narrow layout without script errors or horizontal overflow. Source histories remain unchanged in the repository.

# Paired peninsula / freestanding fit test

The new shaped connection clears the captured bottom tread, so the former stair collision is not a general reason to discard continuity. **This particular connected placement is nevertheless too restrictive to select:** it closes the west approach, leaves a 17.2-inch local gap at the cleanup-side free end, and narrows the gap beside the chef island to 34.7 inches. The freestanding comparison retains a 34.3-inch west approach but has the same eastern and occupied-chair constraints. Neither establishes the requested comfortable four-guest plan.

This is a controlled comparison: the four-place surface, independent helper, island, cleanup, table and body assumptions are identical. Only the shaped connection changes. It answers what continuity costs in this placement; it does not prove that every continuous peninsula fails.

The comparison provides sliders for those assumptions so their effect is explicit. It does not silently turn them into measured appliance specifications.

## Design judgment and next step

Keep F1 as the circulation reference; retain continuity as an unresolved option. Do not select either pictured footprint. The next useful revision is the **guest/helper shape and free-end position**, with a real turning route drawn first, followed by the same connected/disconnected comparison. Simply attaching a link to the prior freestanding shape cannot solve the east-side gate.

No additional chair measurement is needed to conclude that this 17-inch gate needs redesign. No island shrink, reduction to three guests, table move, M5 expansion or primary hall route has been made. Those would be explicit future tradeoffs if a four-place reshaping cannot produce a satisfactory fit.

For the independent appliance-access question, capture the existing refrigerator's relevant open-door/pullout envelope toward the island, and the ovens' actual front/open projection relative to cleanup. Confirm the interpreted 52/120 endpoints only when a specific beverage-room opening depends on them. Do not repeat the S1 ties, general workflow questions or full porch dimensions.

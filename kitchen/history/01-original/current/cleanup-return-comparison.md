## What the geometry establishes

**Shortening alone has a limit.** With the bend at X127, a return must be shorter than 12.5 to end beyond the island's X114.5 side and eliminate their X overlap. A 24-inch DW on that return already exceeds that length before corner and end allowances. Thus a shortened return containing the DW still overlaps this island. It can improve the approach from the stair side; it cannot independently remove the island-end pinch. Moving the DW elsewhere would be an additional reconfiguration.

**A and B are sensitive to the dishwasher's actual location.** The illustrative DW spans X97–121, Y137 at the front. A 27-inch open door reaches Y110. A simple front loading position 20 inches deep then occupies Y90–110 and overlaps the island over Y90–98 and X97–114.5. This flags that particular stance, not every possible side-loading posture. The actual DW position and open projection have been requested to replace the assumptions. The dishwasher's body, corner cabinet, sink access and door hardware need checking together.

**C changes the direction of obstruction.** Its DW door occupies X130–157, Y110–134 under the same 27-inch assumption; an illustrative loader occupies X110–130 over the same Y interval. Neither overlaps the tested island, which ends at Y98. That is a local non-collision result, not a continuous route clearance: a person turning around the island still encounters the loader. The sink-side operator also occupies the 42.5-inch aisle. An additional helper cannot be inserted there simply because two 20-inch body rectangles narrowly fit.

**An island adjustment remains possible in A/B.** Moving the entire 60-inch island six inches toward the appliance wall changes its Y range to 32–92 and the closed return gap to 45; a 27-inch door still leaves only 18 to the end. An eight-inch move makes Y30–90 and leaves 20 at the test door, with no extra margin for the illustrated stance. Both shifts require the uncaptured appliance-wall/open-door envelope; neither is a fit solution. Shortening the island instead would remove part of the current 18/24/18 chef sequence unless that sequence is redesigned.

**The straight-run length is conditional on function size.** The 96-inch test uses a 30-inch sink module. Keeping the same 36 dirty and 30 right-side allowance with a 36-inch sink module makes it 102 long, ending at Y143. A larger actual rack might also change the test. Sink-module width must not be confused with bowl/cutout width.

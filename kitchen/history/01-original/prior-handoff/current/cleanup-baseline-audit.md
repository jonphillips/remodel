## Correction to my earlier landing arithmetic

The prior statement that 65 minus a 30-inch sink module minus a 24-inch dishwasher leaves only 11 inches for landing was misleading. Eleven inches is the base-run length outside those two modules. Countertop above the dishwasher can be usable landing.

In the actual v2 module positions, there are 2 inches before the sink module and 33 inches after it, including the dishwasher’s 24-inch section. That gives 35 inches outside the sink-module reservation, heavily concentrated on one side. These are schematic module lengths, not measured usable counter: the actual bowl, rim, faucet and obstructions are unselected. A sink cabinet’s width is also not identical to its cutout width.

The criticism of the layout still stands, for the correct reason: it never demonstrated simultaneous dirty landing, washing, hand-wash drying and unloading within the reduced space. It also lost the rest of the bent run without accounting for displaced functions. The dishwasher top can count once as a real surface; it cannot simultaneously hold a drying rack and an incoming stack of dishes occupying the same patch.

# Kitchen Reference Images — selected set

These copies are retained because each explains a spatial condition that a floor plan alone does not. They are visual context, not a source of survey dimensions.

## 1. Cleanup diagonal and island relationship

![Cleanup diagonal and island context](reference_photos/cleanup-diagonal-island-context.jpg)

**File:** `reference_photos/cleanup-diagonal-island-context.jpg`  
**Why retain it:** Best wide view of the existing island, the diagonal main-sink cleanup counter, the straight family-room-side counter, and the raised guest-facing edge. It establishes the physical character of the bent cleanup perimeter and why the diagonal is an important design variable.

**Do not infer from it:** exact diagonal angle, counter length, island clearance, or projected alignments.

## 2. Family-room-to-dining circulation context

![Family room to dining circulation context](reference_photos/family-room-to-dining-circulation-context.jpg)

**File:** `reference_photos/family-room-to-dining-circulation-context.jpg`  
**Why retain it:** Shows the current family-room-side passage leading toward dining, the outer face of the cleanup/guest counter, and why that route must be preserved as a natural everyday movement path rather than treated as leftover space.

**Do not infer from it:** a reliable clear width, precise wall location, or table clearance; perspective makes those unsuitable for scaled use.

## Handling rule

Use these photos to check topology, visual massing, and lived circulation. Use the coordinate record and direct field measurements for any plan or eventual 3D model.
